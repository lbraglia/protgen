## Data Protection

Azienda USL-IRCCS of Reggio Emilia will process the personal data of the
subjects participating in the study as autonomous Data Controller, pursuant to
and for the purposes of the European regulations set forth in GDPR 679/2016
and, insofar as currently applicable, in Legislative Decree 196/2003.


Azienda USL-IRCCS is the owner of the data collected in clinical practice and
for the use of patients' personal and sensitive data for research purposes, the
express consent of the person concerned will be requested, as prescribed by the
legislation, on the basis of documents approved by the competent Ethics
Committee.
The data will be used exclusively for the purposes of carrying out this study.


The Scientific Manager of the study is identified as the Delegate for the
processing of personal data, in accordance with the Resolution of the Director
General no. 284/2018, and will appoint with a specific written deed the other
collaborators as persons authorized to process personal data under the
authority of the data controller and the processing delegate, in accordance
with GDPR 679/2016.

Each patient will be informed orally and in writing about the characteristics
of the study, objectives, methods, potential risks and benefits, and must sign
and date the informed consent before participating. No data collection or
clinical evaluation may be performed before written consent has been
obtained. By signing the consent, the patient will authorize the processing of
his/her data for research purposes. The process of delivering the information
sheet and obtaining consent must be documented in the patient's medical
record. A copy of the signed information sheet and consent must be given to the
patient and the original filed at the study center, available for any
checks/inspections.
