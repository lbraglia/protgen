La struttura seguente ricalca la guideline di reporting GRRAS (i numeri tra parentesi graffe, che si consiglia di lasciare, indicano gli item della stessa); ai fini della compilazione delle parti di propria competenza si consiglia di fare riferimento all’[articolo relativo](https://linkinghub.elsevier.com/retrieve/pii/S0895-4356(10)00097-1)

# Title/Abstract {1}

Identify in title or abstract that interrater/intrarater reliability or agreement was investigated.

# Introduction

## Diagnostic/measurement devices {2}

Name and describe the diagnostic or measurement device of interest explicitly.

## Subject population {3}

Specify the subject population of interest.

## Rater population {4}

Specify the rater population of interest (if applicable).

## Background/rationale {5}

Describe what is already known about reliability and agreement and provide a rationale for the study (if applicable).

# Methods

## Sample size {6}

Explain how the sample size was chosen. State the determined number of raters, subjects/objects, and replicate observations.

## Sampling method {7}

Describe the sampling method.

## Measurement/rating process {8}

Describe the measurement/rating process (e.g. time interval between repeated measurements, availability of clinical information, blinding).

## Blindness/indipendence {9}

State whether measurements/ratings were conducted independently.

## Statistical analysis {10}

Describe the statistical analysis.

# Results

## Observation numerosity {11}

State the actual number of raters and subjects/objects which were included and the number of replicate observations which were conducted.

## Raters and subjects{12}

Describe the sample characteristics of raters and subjects (e.g. training, experience).

## Estimates {13}

Report estimates of reliability and agreement including measures of statistical uncertainty.

# Discussion {14}

Discuss the practical relevance of results.

# Auxiliary material {15}

Provide detailed results if possible (e.g. online).
