# Trial introduction & background

## Trial background and rationale

Synopsis of trial background and rationale including a brief description of research question and brief justification for undertaking the trial

## Aim(s) of the trial

Clearly and briefly state the main aim(s) of the trial

## Objectives and/or research hypotheses of the trial

Describe specific trial objectives (primary and secondary) or trial hypotheses

## Trial population

Describe the trial inclusion and exclusion criteria

## Intervention(s) and comparator(s)

Describe the intervention(s) and comparator(s)

## Trial design

Briefly describe the trial design including type of trial such as cluster, crossover, etc. Can also include details of power calculation, sample size (including any separate calculations for economic endpoints), randomisation and blinding.

## Trial start and end dates

Trial recruitment start and end dates and the follow-up period

# Economic approach/overview

## Aim(s) of economic evaluation

Describe the aim(s) of the economic evaluation

## Objective(s) of economic evaluation

Describe the objectives (primary and secondary) of the economic evaluation

## Overview of economic analysis

Briefly outline and justify the type of economic evaluation to be undertaken, identifying the primary economic analysis and outlining the analysis plan and the methods that will be used

## Jurisdiction(s)

Specify the jurisdiction(s) in which the analysis will be conducted including details of the country(s) and health system(s)

## Perspective(s)

State the perspective(s) from which the economic analysis is being conducted, such as societal perspective and/or healthcare payer perspective

## Time horizon(s)

State the time horizon(s) over which costs and consequences are being evaluated

# Economic data collection & management

## Statistical software

Specify the statistical software that will be used to carry out the health economic analysis

## Identification of resources

Justify and describe items of resource use that will be measured as part of the trial

## Measurement of resource-use data

Describe the resource-use data collection method(s) (including external routine datasets) and the time points at which they will be used.

## Valuation of resource-use data

For each resource item measured, describe how the unit cost will be derived and from which specific price year. Outline how adjustments will be made for sources from different price years and which inflation index will be used.

## Identification of outcome(s)

Specify and justify the outcome(s) that will be measured

## Measurement of outcome(s)

Describe the outcome data collection method(s) and the time points at which they will be used

## Valuation of outcome(s)

For each outcome measured, describe how it will be valued and the source of these valuations

## Monitoring collection of health economic data (OPTIONAL)

Outline how the health economic data collected will be monitored

## Database management (OPTIONAL)

Outline how the economic data will be stored and managed and by whom

## Data entry (OPTIONAL)

Outline how data will be entered/handled and outline any checking systems in place

## Data archiving (OPTIONAL)

State whether datasets, interim datasets and final analysis will be archived, and if so, how

# Economic data analysis

## Analysis population

Outline the analysis population that will be used in the economic base-case analysis (such as intention to treat, per protocol)

## Timing of analyses

Describe the timing of all planned analyses (e.g. interim and final analyses)

## Discount rates for costs and benefits

Detail the source of, and justification for, discount rates used for costs and benefits

## Cost-effectiveness threshold(s)

Detail the cost-effectiveness threshold(s) to be used in analysis/interpretation

## Statistical decision rule(s)

Describe how inference will be drawn (e.g. significance level, confidence intervals or mean net benefit)

## Analysis of resource use

Describe how differences in the use of resources/services between randomised groups will be compared

## Analysis of costs

Describe analyses of the cost data, specifying any covariates for statistical adjustment, assumptions, and alternative methods

## Analysis of outcomes

For each outcome used in the economic analysis, describe how the outcome will be analysed, specifying any covariates for statistical adjustment, assumptions, and alternative methods.

## Data cleaning for analysis

Outline how data will be cleaned before analysis

## Missing data

Specify the procedure for dealing with missing data

## Analysis of cost-effectiveness

Describe the methods that will be used to summarise cost-effectiveness.

## Sampling uncertainty

Describe how uncertainty around the costs and effectiveness estimates and summary cost-effectiveness measures will be explored

## Subgroup analyses or analysis of heterogeneity

Describe any analyses of subgroups or heterogeneity in cost-effectiveness and the analysis methods used

## Sensitivity analyses

Describe any sensitivity analyses and their form

# Modelling

## Extrapolation or decision analytic modelling

Outline whether decision analytic modelling or any other extrapolation will be used to estimate cost-effectiveness results beyond the period of the trial or to introduce an additional comparator or other evidence.

## Model type

Describe the modelling approach that will be used and duration of extrapolation

## Model structure

Detail the model structure (where possible, include diagram of model states and transitions between them)

## Treatment effect beyond the end of the trial

Describe the duration and size of treatment effect in the period beyond the end of the trial

## Other key assumptions

List the key structural assumptions of the model

## Methods for identifying and estimating parameters

For each model parameter, describe the methods and data sources that will be used to estimate the parameter (e.g. from the RCT, systematic review, meta-analysis, other published data or expert opinion)

## Model uncertainty

Describe the methods that will be used to assess parameter uncertainty in the results. Describe sensitivity analyses for the impact of other types of uncertainty on results.

## Model validation

Describe the methods and data that will be used to check the face, internal and external validity of the model

## Subgroup analyses/heterogeneity

Describe subgroup or heterogeneity analyses that will be executed and reported within the extrapolation or decision analytic modelling

## Value of information analysis (OPTIONAL)

Describe whether value of information analysis is planned and the type and methods that will be used to calculate value of information

# Reporting/publishing

## Reporting standards

Describe any guidelines that will be followed when publishing results

## Deviations from the HEAP

Describe the procedure for reporting any deviations from the HEAP

# Appendices

## Health economic collection tools

Include template examples of the resource-use data collection sheets and resource-use questionnaires

## Cross-referencing to other trial documents (OPTIONAL)

Reference to other relevant trial documents that are adhered to and followed when writing the HEAP and any other references used when writing the HEAP

## Illustrations (OPTIONAL)

Illustrations such as annotated questionnaires detailing the database fieldnames, flow charts outlining the flow of data for the economic evaluation, or template tables
